GST amount calculator

Split an amount into taxable value and tax, or add tax on top. Slabs follow GST 2.0 (from 22 Sep 2025): 0%, 5%, 18% and 40%, plus 3% and 0.25% for special goods. The old 12% and 28% slabs no longer exist; confirm the rate for the item or HSN before invoicing.

Late fee and interest on GST returns

Late fee under section 47 is per day of delay up to a cap set by turnover. Interest under section 50 is 18% a year on tax paid late. Rates and caps are standard notified figures; check for any waiver or amnesty before quoting a client.

GSTR-1 summary

One sale per line: buyer GSTIN (blank for consumers), invoice, taxable value, rate %, and optionally I in a fifth field for an inter-state consumer sale. Splits B2B and B2C and works out CGST, SGST and IGST by rate.

TDS deducted vs deposited

Deposit is due by the 7th of the next month (30 April for March). Enter deductions as date, section, TDS amount and challans as date, challan no, amount. Late deposit interest is 1.5% a month on the unpaid or late amount, part of a month counting as a month.

Depreciation on sale of a block

Income tax block of assets. Sale proceeds above the block value give a short-term gain; a block that ends with a balance gives a short-term loss; otherwise depreciation runs on the reduced value, at half rate for assets used under 180 days.

TDS return working (24Q / 26Q)

One line per deduction: deductee, PAN, section, payment date, amount paid, TDS deducted, deposit date (leave the last blank if not deposited). Groups by quarter, shows the filing due date and flags missing PAN, short deduction and late deposit. This is a working for checking; prepare the actual return in the NSDL/TIN utility.

Income tax, old vs new regime (FY 2026-27)

Individuals. Slabs are unchanged from FY 2025-26. Special-rate income such as capital gains is not included here.

New regime: standard deduction ₹75,000, rebate up to ₹60,000 for taxable income up to ₹12 lakh with marginal relief just above it, surcharge capped at 25%. Old regime: standard deduction ₹50,000, rebate up to ₹12,500 for taxable income up to ₹5 lakh, surcharge up to 37%. Cess 4%. Surcharge marginal relief is applied.
