Due-date calendar

Every filing for every client, laid out by date. Assign each filing to a team member, tick it off, and send reminders to the client or the staff member on WhatsApp or email with one click. Filings come from the clients and filings you pick in the Compliance tracker.

Filings this month

"Client" opens WhatsApp with a ready message to the client's mobile from the Client master. "Staff" sends the task to the person it is assigned to. Messages open in your own WhatsApp or email, so nothing is sent without you pressing Send.

Set up filings automatically

Adds every client from the Client master to the Compliance tracker with the usual filings for their type, read from the PAN and GSTIN: a company (4th letter C) gets ROC forms and audit ITR, a firm or LLP (F) gets audit ITR and LLP forms, an individual (P) gets the non-audit ITR, and any client with a GSTIN gets monthly GSTR-1 and 3B. Clients already in the tracker are left as they are. You can edit each one afterwards.

Message templates

Use {client}, {filing}, {period}, {due}, {docs}, {firm} and {staff}. Saved for the whole firm.

Team and logins

Each person signs in with their own email and password. Partner sees everything and manages the team. Staff uses every tool and sees fees. Article uses every tool but does not see fees, rates and settings, or backup.

Fee invoices and outstanding

Raise GST invoices for your fees, record payments (including TDS the client deducted), and follow up on what is outstanding. Invoice numbers run in a series per financial year.

Outstanding by client

Firm details used on invoices

Time and work

Record time on each client with the timer or by hand. Partners see who is working on what, what is overdue, and how the time compares with the fees billed.

My time

Client document inbox

Give each client a private upload link. They open it on their phone or computer, pick files (bills, bank statements, Form 16) and press Upload; no login needed. The files arrive here for the whole team.
